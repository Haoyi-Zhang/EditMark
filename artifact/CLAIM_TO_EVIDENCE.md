# Claim-to-Evidence Map

This file is the artifact-side reading guide for the submitted paper and supplement. It tells a reviewer which shipped objects support each paper-level claim and which stronger claims are intentionally not made.

## Paper Table and File Binding

- The main method-profile table is bound to `results/tables/suite_all_models_methods/method_summary.*`.
- The headline detection-to-robustness gap is bound to `artifact/headline_gap_statistics.*` and `results/tables/suite_all_models_methods/model_method_summary.*`. The rounded static table recomputes to 0.3221; the paper reports 0.3220 from the frozen unrounded analysis. Leave-one-out recomputation keeps the mean gap between 0.3093 and 0.3327, showing that no single method-by-generator slice dominates the aggregate.
- Core and stress transformation evidence is bound to `results/tables/suite_all_models_methods/per_attack_robustness_breakdown.*` and `core_vs_stress_robustness_summary.*`.
- Support, control, and claim-boundary evidence is bound to `results/tables/suite_all_models_methods/gate_decomposition.*` and `robustness_factor_decomposition.*`.
- Figures under `results/figures/` are reviewer-facing summaries of those tables, not a separate result source.

The four evaluated baselines are a reproducible comparison surface rather than a population sample. They were retained because each exposes the pieces needed for a common post-edit provenance test: generation, a declared detector and threshold, transformed-code validation, negative controls, support, and cost. Methods outside that boundary may be important future targets, but they cannot remove the paper's central proxy test: whether clean detector evidence remains admissible after test-passing software edits.

The intended review sequence is source admission, method surface, transformation surface, control and support checks, then claim interpretation. Reading the tables in that order prevents a clean-detection result from being silently promoted into a post-edit provenance guarantee.
