# Dataset surface

The included release inventory has seven source groups: HumanEval+, MBPP+, HumanEval-X, MBXP, and three locally constructed slices for original, translation, and stress tasks. The source files and their exact counts are listed in `analysis/source_inventory.csv` and `analysis/frozen_audit.json`.

The constructed slices are controlled benchmark material, not independently peer-reviewed datasets. Their template-family, category, and difficulty metadata are descriptive design records. The paper avoids treating this inventory as evidence that generated programs succeeded or that the task oracles are complete.
